# Contributing / 参与讨论

CEP is a public draft. Contributions are welcome when they improve clarity, falsifiability, corrigibility, reversibility, evaluation design, or civilization-level resilience.

CEP 是公开草案。欢迎围绕以下方向提出修改建议：可证伪性、可纠错性、可回滚性、评价体系、异质监督、文明级韧性。

## Suggested contribution format / 建议提案格式

Please include:

1. The clause you want to change / 希望修改的条款
2. The failure mode you are addressing / 试图解决的失败模式
3. Why the current wording is insufficient / 现有表述为什么不足
4. Your proposed revision / 建议修改文本
5. A counterargument against your own proposal / 对自己提案的最强反对意见
6. What evidence would change your mind / 什么证据会让你改变观点

The goal is not to maximize agreement. The goal is to make the protocol harder to fool.

目标不是最大化共识，而是让这套协议更难被欺骗。
