# CEP 2.3 v5.1 | Public Discussion and Review Closeout

As of October 10, 2026, CEP 2.3 v5.1 is published as a public discussion draft. The current cycle of third-party tabletop-tool reviews is closed; this is **not law, an adopted standard, or an independent security certification**.

## Main idea

As AI capabilities advance, systems and institutions should preserve corrigibility, accountability, meaningful human choice, independent scrutiny, and avenues for appeal. The draft discusses high-impact decisions, whistleblower protection, substantive audit independence, and strict limits on interpreting CEP as authorization for agents to acquire privileges.

## Evidence and remaining limits

An independent report recorded 37 new blind-test passes against tabletop tool v2.1. The later v2.1.1 fixed a manifest-type validation bypass; its 37/37 rerun of the earlier third-party cases and 8/8 targeted fix tests were carried out by a project-associated party. They are **not** independent formal approval of v2.1.1 or evidence of real-world security. Real whistleblower anonymity, auditor independence, production agent permissions, and legal enforceability remain untested.

Public, versioned clause-specific criticism remains welcome through GitHub Issues and Pull Requests. No further simulated pass score is required simply to publish the discussion draft.
